# Nightfall website direction

User-approved world: softly illustrated alpine winter, detailed rather than flat cartoon artwork. Snow-white, sky blue and pine green by day; peach and warm ivory at evening; deep navy and cool snow at night. Warm amber lights and restrained cranberry accents remain consistent.

Typography direction: expressive editorial serif headline with a readable humanist sans for navigation and controls. Spacious hierarchy inspired by the user's Aethera recording. Real installation actions remain legible semantic controls.

Surface mode: Persuade. First screen communicates free desktop snow for macOS and Hyprland. A broad landscape anchors the composition; headline and primary action occupy quiet sky. Named time phases are keyboard-operable. Future snow and scroll animation must respect reduced motion.

Stage 1: composition, responsive hero, typography and time-of-day palettes. No full character choreography or accumulation in this milestone.
